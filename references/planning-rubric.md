# Planning Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 100.

Execution may continue only when every dimension is above 80.

## Dimensions

### Scientific clarity

Can a downstream agent clearly tell what scientific message the figure must express?

### Task completeness

Does the plan include the major required outputs, panels, modules, and relationships?

### Element definition clarity

Are the required visual elements sufficiently specified?

### Layout sufficiency

Does the plan contain enough layout guidance to avoid arbitrary composition later?

### Style specificity

Are style constraints concrete enough to guide `drawer`?

### Copy specificity

Are text expectations concrete enough to guide `writer`?

### Asset constraint clarity

If assets exist, are their semantic and structural constraints documented well enough?

### Draw.io executability

Can the plan realistically be translated into editable `draw.io` XML?

### Internal consistency

Do the constraints fit together without contradiction?

### Risk controllability

Are the remaining unknowns limited and manageable?

## Failure Reporting

For every score at or below 80, explain:

- the problem
- why it matters
- the next best clarification target
